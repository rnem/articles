There's no linear path. Each fix unlocks the next problem. You have to triage, prioritize, and execute under real time pressure.

What makes these AWS microcredentials different from traditional certifications is that there's nowhere to hide. You can't reason your way through multiple choice. Either you can operate in a live environment or you can't.
